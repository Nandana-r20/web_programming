About Us

COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING CHENGNNUR is a preimer college which provide high standard education. he College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) is a well-known government-backed engineering institution established in 1993 in Alappuzha, Kerala. Managed by the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD), the college is affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). It offers popular undergraduate and postgraduate programs focused heavily on technology, computing, and electronic engineering disciplines.

courses offered

The College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) offers full-time undergraduate and postgraduate programs across technical and computer application disciplines. For undergraduate students, the institution provides four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degrees in core branches like Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, and Electrical & Electronics Engineering. It also supports direct B.Tech Lateral Entry pathways for diploma holders looking to enter straight into the second year of engineering. At the postgraduate level, the college features specialized two-year Master of Technology (M.Tech) programs, with focuses such as VLSI & Embedded Systems and Digital Image Processing. Additionally, students looking for advanced IT careers can enroll in the highly popular Master of Computer Applications (MCA) degree program.

College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) B.Tech seats are distributed primarily through KEAM Centralised Allotment Process (CAP) based on a 50:50 weightage of KEAM scores and Class 12 board marks.

Merit Lower Fee (50% of seats): Allotted by CEE Kerala strictly on KEAM merit at standard government-subsidized fees.Merit Full Fee / Management (35% of seats): Also allotted by CEE Kerala via KEAM merit, but at a higher tuition fee rate.NRI Quota (15% of seats): Filled directly by the IHRD management for eligible NRI dependents.
